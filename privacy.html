Privacy Policy

Black Ninja Cowboy · Effective Date: 20.08.2026 · Last Updated: 20.08.2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Black Ninja Cowboy ("Game"), developed by Gábor Balázs ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our"), collects and uses limited gameplay statistics.

Black Ninja Cowboy is a free, single-player indie game. The Game does not require an account and does not contain multiplayer functionality, in-game purchases, DLC, loot boxes, or advertising.

1. Information We Collect

When you play Black Ninja Cowboy, the Game may send limited gameplay statistics to the Developer.

The information collected consists of:

  • the date and time when the gameplay data is received;
  • a session identifier generated from the session timestamp;
  • the duration of the gameplay session, measured in seconds;
  • the duration of the gameplay session, measured in minutes; and
  • the status of the gameplay session.

The Game does not intentionally collect or transmit:

  • your name;
  • email address;
  • password;
  • account information;
  • payment information;
  • precise geographical location;
  • IP address;
  • Steam ID or other platform account identifiers;
  • advertising identifiers;
  • device identifiers;
  • contacts;
  • photographs;
  • camera or microphone data;
  • chat messages; or
  • other information intended to directly identify you.

The Game does not require you to create an account or provide personal information in order to play.

2. Session Identifier

When gameplay statistics are sent, the Game generates a session identifier using the timestamp at which the data is sent.

The session identifier is used to distinguish individual gameplay records in the Developer's statistics. It is not a permanent player identifier and is not intentionally used to identify individual players.

The Game does not store a permanent player ID or account ID for analytics purposes.

3. Why We Collect This Data

The gameplay statistics are collected solely to understand how players use Black Ninja Cowboy and to improve the Game. This may include analyzing:

  • how often the Game is launched;
  • how long players play;
  • average session duration;
  • overall player engagement;
  • general gameplay patterns; and
  • the effects of gameplay changes and future updates.

The collected information is not used for advertising, targeted marketing, or selling information about players.

4. How the Data Is Transmitted

The Game sends the gameplay statistics to a Google Apps Script Web App operated by the Developer. The Web App receives the gameplay information and records it in a Google Sheets spreadsheet controlled by the Developer.

The Game does not intentionally send an IP address or other directly identifying information as part of the gameplay data. The Developer's script is designed to process only the gameplay information described in this Privacy Policy.

5. Where the Data Is Stored

The collected gameplay statistics are stored in a Google Sheets spreadsheet. The analytics data is not stored as an analytics database on the player's computer.

The Developer may periodically create backup copies of the spreadsheet and store those backups locally on the Developer's computer. The spreadsheet and local backups are not publicly available and are intended to be accessible only to the Developer.

6. Google Services

Google Apps Script and Google Sheets are third-party services provided by Google and are used to transmit and store the gameplay statistics. Google may process information in connection with providing these services according to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

The Developer does not use Google services to identify individual players, display advertising to players, or create advertising profiles.

For information about Google's processing of information, please refer to Google's applicable privacy documentation.

7. Data Sharing

The Developer does not sell, rent, or commercially share the collected gameplay statistics with third parties.

The data may be processed by Google as necessary to provide the Google Apps Script and Google Sheets services.

The Developer may disclose information if required by applicable law or by a lawful request from a competent authority.

8. Data Retention

The Developer intends to retain gameplay statistics only for as long as reasonably necessary to analyze the Game's usage and improve Black Ninja Cowboy. The Developer may periodically review and delete data that is no longer reasonably necessary for these purposes.

Local backup copies are subject to the same general retention principles.

9. Legal Basis

If the gameplay statistics are considered personal data under applicable data-protection law, the Developer's intended legal basis for processing is legitimate interest, namely the legitimate interest in developing, maintaining, testing, balancing, and improving Black Ninja Cowboy.

The Developer limits the information collected to gameplay statistics reasonably necessary for these purposes.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable data-protection law and whether the information can be associated with you, you may have rights including:

  • the right to request access to personal data;
  • the right to request correction of inaccurate personal data;
  • the right to request deletion;
  • the right to object to certain processing;
  • the right to restrict processing; and
  • other rights provided by applicable law.

Because the Game does not intentionally collect information such as your name or email address, the Developer may not be able to identify a particular player or associate a gameplay record with a specific individual.

11. Children

Black Ninja Cowboy does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

The Game does not require players to create an account or provide personal information.

12. Data Security

The Developer takes reasonable measures to protect the collected gameplay statistics against unauthorized access, accidental loss, destruction, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the Game's data-collection practices change or if an update is necessary to reflect applicable legal requirements.

If the Developer begins collecting additional types of information, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe that collection.

The latest version of this Privacy Policy will be made available through the Game's website or distribution page.
