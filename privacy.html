Privacy Policy

Black Ninja Cowboy · Effective Date: 20.08.2026 · Last Updated: 28.09.2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Black Ninja Cowboy ("Game"), developed by Gábor Balázs ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our"), handles data. The Game collects and uses limited gameplay statistics. However, we do not want, collect, or store your personal data.

Black Ninja Cowboy is a free, single-player indie game. The Game does not require an account and does not contain multiplayer functionality, in-game purchases, DLC, or loot boxes. The versions distributed through the Microsoft Store, Xbox Store and itch.io do not contain advertising. The Android version distributed through Google Play displays ads provided by Google AdMob, as described in section 8.

1. Information We Collect

When you play Black Ninja Cowboy, the Game sends limited gameplay statistics to the Developer.

The information collected consists of:

  • the date and time when the gameplay data is received;
  • a session identifier generated from the session timestamp;
  • the duration of the gameplay session, measured in seconds;
  • the duration of the gameplay session, measured in minutes; and
  • the status of the gameplay session.

The Game's own gameplay statistics do not include, and the Developer does not collect, store or receive:

  • real names;
  • usernames;
  • email addresses;
  • password;
  • account information;
  • payment information;
  • precise geographical location;
  • IP address;
  • Steam ID or other platform account identifiers;
  • advertising identifiers;
  • device identifiers;
  • contacts;
  • photographs;
  • camera or microphone data;
  • chat messages; or
  • other information intended to directly identify you.

The Game does not require you to create an account or provide personal information in order to play.

The list above applies to the Game's own gameplay statistics. In the Google Play version, the Google AdMob advertising service may separately process some of this kind of information, as described in section 8.

2. Local Save States

Any game settings, options, or progress checkpoints you make are saved strictly on your own device. This data remains entirely under your personal control and is never transmitted to us.

3. Session Identifier

When gameplay statistics are sent, the Game generates a session identifier using the timestamp at which the data is sent.

This data is completely anonymous and aggregated. For the gameplay statistics, we do not use user tracking tokens, cookies, or session histories. The statistics are mixed into a collective pool of numbers, making it mathematically impossible for us to isolate, identify, or track any individual person. No database of tracking or analytics data is ever saved or compiled on your local computer. The Game does not store a permanent player ID or account ID for analytics purposes.

The session identifier is used to distinguish individual gameplay records in the Developer's statistics.

4. Why We Collect This Data

The gameplay statistics are collected solely to understand how players use Black Ninja Cowboy and to improve the Game.

This may include analyzing:

  • how often the Game is launched;
  • how long players play;
  • average session duration;
  • overall player engagement;
  • general gameplay patterns; and
  • the effects of gameplay changes and future updates.

The collected gameplay statistics are not used for advertising, targeted marketing, or selling information about players.

5. How the Data Is Transmitted

The Game sends the gameplay statistics to a Google Apps Script Web App operated by the Developer.

The Web App receives the gameplay information and records it in a Google Sheets spreadsheet controlled by the Developer.

The Game does not send an IP address or other directly identifying information as part of the gameplay data.

The Developer's script is specifically designed to process only the gameplay information described in this Privacy Policy.

6. Where the Data Is Stored

The collected gameplay statistics are stored in a Google Sheets spreadsheet.

The analytics data is not stored as an analytics database on the player's computer.

The Developer may periodically create backup copies of the spreadsheet and store those backups locally on the Developer's computer.

The spreadsheet and local backups are not publicly available and are accessible only to the Developer itself.

7. Google Services

Google Apps Script and Google Sheets are third-party services provided by Google and are used to transmit and store the gameplay statistics.

Google may process information in connection with providing these services according to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

The Developer does not use Google Apps Script or Google Sheets to identify individual players, display advertising to players, or create advertising profiles.

For information about Google's processing of information, please refer to Google's applicable privacy documentation.

8. Advertising (Google Play Version Only)

The Android version of the Game distributed through Google Play displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. The versions distributed through the Microsoft Store, Xbox Store and itch.io do not display advertisements and do not use any advertising service.

To load and show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK included in the Game may collect and process information from your device, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and operating system information, ad interaction data (for example impressions and clicks), and approximate location derived from your IP address. This information is collected and processed by Google, not by the Developer, and may be used to serve, measure, and personalize ads. Google's processing of this information is governed by Google's own terms and privacy policy.

The Developer does not receive this information and cannot identify you through it. Through AdMob, the Developer can only see aggregated reports, such as the number of ad impressions and clicks and estimated earnings. The Game does not include any other advertising or tracking tools, and ad-related data is not connected to the gameplay statistics described in this Privacy Policy.

You can control ad personalization on your Android device, for example by deleting your advertising ID or opting out of ad personalization in your device's Google or Privacy settings under "Ads" (the exact location varies by device). For more information, please see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

9. Data Sharing

The Developer does not sell, rent, or commercially share the collected gameplay statistics with third parties.

The data may be processed by Google as necessary to provide the Google Apps Script and Google Sheets services.

Information processed by Google AdMob in the Google Play version is collected and handled by Google under its own policies. The Developer does not receive personal data from AdMob and does not share it with anyone.

The Developer may disclose information if required by applicable law or by a lawful request from a competent authority.

10. Data Retention

The Developer intends to retain gameplay statistics only for as long as reasonably necessary to analyze the Game's usage and improve Black Ninja Cowboy.

The Developer may periodically review and delete data that is no longer reasonably necessary for these purposes.

Local backup copies are subject to the same general retention principles.

11. Data Security

The anonymous gameplay records are transmitted using secure, encrypted connections (HTTPS) and are stored in a private, restricted Google Sheets database. Access to this spreadsheet is strictly limited to authorized developers via multi-factor authentication to ensure the data cannot be modified, intercepted, or accessed by unauthorized third parties.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the Game's data-collection practices change or if an update is necessary to reflect applicable legal requirements.

If the Developer begins collecting additional types of information, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe that collection.

The latest version of this Privacy Policy will be made available through the Game's website or distribution page.
