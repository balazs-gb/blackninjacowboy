About the game

Meet Black Ninja Cowboy:

Fast, chaotic bullet-hell action with roguelike runs — no two dungeons play the same A revenge story wrapped in dark, sarcastic humor and nonsense dialogue Clear every dungeon, defeat every enemy, and uncover who's really behind the clan war Pixel art carnage designed for easy pickup and genuine challenge Random absurd events keep every run unpredictable

Guide to the game: You start with the base map, where you can explore the world, enter different dungeons, talk to NPCs, and upgrade your gear. There are two types of dungeons: one with many different enemies, where you earn gold by eliminating them, and another where there is only one boss. You won't get gold there, but by defeating the boss, you get a key to unlock the next dungeon. The gathered gold can be used to upgrade your weapons in different workshops. Each weapon has its own place to upgrade. You start with your revolver, but you can unlock many more weapons by talking to the NPCs. You can track your weapon stats in the journal, as well as your progress in finding all the weapons. You can find collectible items in each dungeon, and you can track your all-time stats at the bounty board near the saloon. (this is my very first video game, it is similar to the Vampire Survivor, but more fun and not serious at all)

Behind the scenes

How it's made

This game was made in Gdevelop, a free and open-source NO-CODE game engine. It's a solo development project, and I implemented everything myself: programming, pixel art (own creations and AI creations too), music (AI-generated tracks and Pixabay musics), sound effects, and marketing. I learned a lot during the development process, and I hope to continue improving my skills and making more games in the future.
